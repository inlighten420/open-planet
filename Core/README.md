# Geodrive Core

Engine-free C++ shared by the game, the tools and (later) other engines such as Godot. No Unreal types, no exceptions, no CPU-specific code.

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `GeoTiles/` | The packed map tile format: OSM ways in z14 tiles, zstd per tile, in one PMTiles file. See [docs/TILE_FORMAT_TEST.md](../docs/TILE_FORMAT_TEST.md). |
| `GeoRoads/` | The road network (`RoadGraph.h`: nodes, roads, plain `Vec2` points in cm; `RoadNetwork`: built from OSM ways, widths/lanes/shoulders/speeds by SA or UK rules, bridges, tunnels, tapers; `Projection.h`: lat/lon to world cm), the road design rules (`RoadDesign`: grades, crest/sag K, landings by class and speed, SANRAL / Red Book) the road height solver (`RoadHeights`: smooth network solve, grade limits with an earthworks budget, bridges lifted to clear the roads they cross) and the shared road surface (`RoadSurface`: one height at any point, blended from the centrelines nearby, so every road and junction piece agrees). The host passes its own ParallelFor and log; `ThreadsParallelFor` is there for hosts without a job system. |
| `GeoGround/` | The ground: `HeightRaster` (Terrarium SRTM tiles stitched, Copernicus base heights as the lower per pixel, LiDAR fine heights blended in at their edge, sampled bilinear; the host fetches and decodes the tiles and maps the archives).  `Terrain`: the ground builder (land cover, forest canopy, road profiles, corridors with verges, kerbs, cuttings and embankments, the grid and its skirts, distant blocks, water, tunnel mouths); `Mesh`: plain mesh data laid out as the engines' vectors. |
| `GeoMesh/` | Road and building meshes: junction surfaces and ribbons on the shared road surface, skirts, gap paving between roads side by side, rounded dead ends, painted lines (SA / UK rules, built in chain order so the same every run), buildings fitted round roads, open galleries and tunnel tubes. |
| `GeoCar/` | The cars: `CarSound` (the engine, tyres and wind synthesised live: each cylinder firing into its exhaust pipe, turbo, pops; both games play it), `CarBody` / `CarShapes` / `CarDesigns` (bodies made in code: lofts, wheels, wings, tubes; the GT, Formula, Hatchback and Bakkie with their wheels, cameras, setups and engine sounds), `CarSim` (suspension sweeps, spring / damper / bump stop / anti-roll bar, Ackermann steering, engine, launch clutch or the driver's clutch with stalling, gearbox with automatic mode, limited slip or locked diff, implicit wheel spin, ABS and traction control, aero, steering torque for force feedback) and `Tire` (TMeasy, ported from Project Chrono, BSD-3: see `GeoCar/ThirdPartyNotices.md`). SI units; the host gives it the body's state and a `CarWorld` (sphere sweeps, forces at a point). |
| `GeoFFB/` | Steering wheels: `WheelSetup` (setting a pedal by pressing it, and the wheel's own buttons for the camera and seat), `WheelDevice` (DirectInput 8: axes, buttons, hats; constant force, damper, friction), `ForceFeedback` (its own ~500 Hz thread: smoothing, soft clip, minimum force, the tyres' effects — lock-up judder, ABS pulses, scrub grain, loose-surface rumble, road bumps — read straight from the car's `CarTelemetry`; forces fade if the game stops sending), `WheelDriver` (the settings a game saves, pedals and calibration, steering through the ratio, paddles, and the force command from the car's state). |
| `GeoPlaces/` | The ready-made areas to drive and their named places (`Areas.h`), for the menus of both games. |
| `GeoTraffic/` | Traffic (`Traffic`): cars on the same physics as the player's driving the roads round them in the left lane at the speed limit, slowing for bends and give-ways, keeping a gap, easing round parked cars, a third parked (some pull out), each a road tester (launches, jolts). The game puts the cars in its world through `TrafficHost`; both games use it. |
| `GeoWorld/` | One piece of the world (`Piece`): the map data read out of a country archive, heights, the road network solved and joined to the piece before at the seam (`GeoRoads/RoadSeams`), the ground builder ready for any chunk, roads / lines / buildings cut into 1 km tiles, water, distant blocks, and for a new area the start and the ground round it. The host streams it in. `RoadScan`: every road driven as the car would feel it (jolts, launches, grass through the road, holes, too steep, dead ends in mid-air, odd ground beside it), summary and CSV; both games use it. `PieceRequest::Stage` says what it starts on next, for a loading screen. |
| `third_party/zstd/` | zstd 1.5.7 (BSD licence). `zstddeclib.c` is zstd's own single-file decoder, generated with `build/single_file_libs/combine.sh`. |

**Who builds it:**
- The game: `Godot/extension/` compiles these files into the GeodriveWorld extension.
- The packer: `Tools/geotile/build.bat`.
